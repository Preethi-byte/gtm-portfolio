The review rules

  • A close date before the reference date prompts a buyer-timing check.
  • A missing or zero amount prompts confirmation of deal value.
  • Missing activity or a gap of at least 21 days prompts an engagement review.

Handle exceptions clearly

Keep all applicable reasons, clear flags when the source values become healthy, and remove closed deals from the review queue. Confirm the activity source before treating a missing date as a useful signal.

Measure whether reviews help

Track preparation time, owner response, and how often a flag leads to a confirmed action. Agree a baseline with Sales and RevOps before setting a target.
